# Additional Blood Bank Questions

Use these questions for individual experiments and the assignment's examples file. Compare the generated SQL, returned rows, and friendly answer; record questions that fail or produce a misleading summary.

1. Which blood type has the most registered donors?
2. Which donors have never donated?
3. How many donations were collected in each city?
4. Which staff members are assigned to more than one location?
5. What is the average donation amount for each blood type?
6. Which donors have a medical condition recorded, and what is its severity?
7. Which donors have made at least two donations, and how many have they made?
8. How many donations were collected in 2025 compared with 2026?
9. Which location has collected the greatest total donation amount?
10. Which donors have a medication recorded?
